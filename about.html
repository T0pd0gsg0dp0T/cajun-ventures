Our Story

BUILT ON HARD WORK & INTEGRITY

Cajun Ventures was founded with a simple mission: deliver exceptional land services with honest pricing and reliable results. What started as helping neighbors with land clearing has grown into a full-service land company serving a 100-mile radius from our home base in Winnie, Texas.

Owner/Operator Jacob Junot brings years of hands-on experience operating heavy equipment and transforming properties across Southeast Texas. When you work with Cajun Ventures, you're working directly with the owner — not a salesman, not a middleman.

We treat every property like it's our own, whether it's a small residential lot or a multi-acre commercial project. That personal touch and attention to detail is what sets us apart.
