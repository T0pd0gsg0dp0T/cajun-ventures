PERMITTING & REGULATIONS

Permit requirements vary by county and municipality. In most rural areas of Chambers, Jefferson, and Liberty Counties, permits are not required for basic land clearing on private property. However, if your project involves:

  • Work near wetlands or designated floodplains
  • Significant fill or grade changes (typically 1+ feet)
  • Drainage modifications affecting neighboring properties
  • Properties within city limits or HOA jurisdictions

You may need permits from the county, city, or Army Corps of Engineers. We can help advise based on your specific location and project scope, but always recommend confirming with local authorities for your situation.

Work within or adjacent to wetlands, streams, or bayous may require Section 404 permits from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. We are experienced in working on properties with wetland considerations and can help identify potential issues during our site evaluation. For large projects in sensitive areas, we recommend consulting with a wetland specialist.

In most rural areas, small stock ponds and private recreational ponds on your own property do not require permits. However, if the pond will dam or redirect natural waterways, or if it exceeds certain size thresholds, permits may be required from the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) or local authorities. We can help you understand what applies to your project.

Timelines

PROJECT DURATION

Project timelines depend on several factors:

  • 1-2 acre residential lot: 1-3 days
  • 5-10 acre clearing: 3-7 days
  • 10-20+ acre commercial: 1-2 weeks

Factors that affect timeline include vegetation density, number and size of trees, terrain accessibility, and weather conditions. We provide specific time estimates with every quote.

Pond construction timelines vary by size and complexity:

  • Small stock pond (1/4 - 1/2 acre): 2-4 days
  • Medium pond (1/2 - 1 acre): 4-7 days
  • Large pond (1+ acre): 1-2 weeks

This includes clearing the area, excavation, grading the banks, and building any necessary dams or spillways. Weather can significantly impact pond construction schedules.

Our scheduling varies based on current workload and season. Typically, we can start smaller projects within 1-2 weeks of approving your quote. Larger projects may require more lead time for scheduling equipment and crew. For urgent needs like storm cleanup, we offer emergency services and can often respond within 24-48 hours.

Pricing

COST FACTORS

Land clearing costs in Southeast Texas typically range from $1,500 to $5,000+ per acre, depending on:

  • Vegetation density: Light brush vs. heavy timber
  • Tree size and quantity: More/larger trees = higher cost
  • Terrain: Flat, dry land vs. wet, uneven ground
  • Debris disposal: On-site burning/piling vs. hauling
  • Stump removal: Grinding or removal adds to cost
  • Accessibility: How easily equipment can access the site

We provide free on-site evaluations to give you an accurate, no-obligation quote for your specific property.
